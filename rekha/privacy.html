Rekha Learning — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Applies to the Rekha Learning app

The short version

Rekha Learning is built for young children, so the honest summary matters more than the legal one:

  • You can use the app without an account at all. Nothing then leaves the device — not progress, not a name, nothing.
  • If you make an account, we store what is needed to carry your child's progress between devices. Nothing more.
  • We do not sell data. We do not show ads. There is no third-party analytics or advertising code in the app.
  • The app never asks for location — it holds no location permission at all.
  • The microphone is used only inside a “say it aloud” activity, only after the child taps to start, and no recording is ever stored or sent anywhere.

A one-page summary of exactly what is and is not collected is on the Data safety page.

Playing without an account

The app can be used as a guest. In guest mode, progress is kept only in the app's own storage on that device, and nothing is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. This mode is complete — the whole curriculum works offline.

What we collect if you create an account

About the grown-up. A name, an email address (required, so the account can be confirmed and recovered), a username, a password, and optionally a phone number. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in readable form.

About the child. A name — a first name or a nickname is entirely sufficient, and we would encourage a nickname — and an age, which is used only to suggest a sensible starting chapter. A chosen avatar (an emoji character) and a left- or right-handed preference.

About their learning. Which lessons were finished and the stars earned, an estimate of how well each skill is going, and a record of finished activities. Each activity record holds the activity's identifier, whether the answer was right, the stars given, and for tracing activities some measurements of the stroke itself — how far it strayed from the guide, how many times the finger lifted, whether a letter was drawn backwards. These exist so the app can adapt the difficulty and so the grown-up's report is truthful.

What stays on the device and is never sent

The chosen language, the daily goal, the screen-time limit and today's usage, and the practice-reminder time are all stored only on that device. They describe how one tablet is used rather than what a child has learned, and syncing them would mean holding data we have no need for.

The microphone

Some activities ask a child to say a word aloud. When one of these runs, and only after the child taps the button to begin:

  • The app asks the operating system to recognise speech on the device itself, so that a child's voice does not travel to a cloud service.
  • The resulting text is compared against the expected word in memory, and then discarded.
  • No audio is recorded to a file. No transcript is written down, logged, stored, or transmitted. Nothing about it is added to the child's record beyond whether the activity was completed.
  • The activity can always be skipped, and skipping never starts the microphone.

One limitation stated plainly: on-device recognition is a request to the operating system, and on some devices the platform may only offer a server-backed recogniser without telling the app which it used. Where that is the case, the speech would be processed by the device manufacturer's or operating system's own speech service under their privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive it either way.

At the time of writing, no lesson in the shipped curriculum uses this activity type, so in practice the microphone is not used at all. The permission is declared for when those lessons are added.

Location

The app requests no location permission and cannot read your GPS position. The optional leaderboard groups children by a city, state or country that a parent types in themselves. Nothing is inferred from IP address or device sensors, and leaving it blank simply means a wider grouping.

The leaderboard, if you turn it on

It is off by default and is only ever switched on by a grown-up behind the parent gate. When it is on:

  • A child appears under a generated handle such as “SwiftMangoTiger”, never under their real name.
  • They are only ranked inside a group large enough that no individual can be picked out; if the group you declared is too small, the app automatically widens it.
  • Nobody is ever shown in last place.
  • Turning it off stops participation.

Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled by the app on the device itself. They are not push notifications, no reminder passes through a server, and we do not learn whether one was seen.

Who else is involved

We use Supabase to host the database and handle sign-in for accounts, and it stores the account data described above on our behalf. Confirmation emails are sent through an email provider. If the app is installed from an app store, that store handles the download and any purchases under its own privacy policy. That is the complete list — there is no advertising network, no analytics service, and no data broker.

How long we keep it

Account and progress data is kept while the account exists, because its whole purpose is to still be there when your child comes back.

You can close the account yourself in the app, which stops it working immediately and blocks all further access to its data — but keeps the record, deactivated, so a mis-tap is recoverable. You can also ask us to erase it, which removes the account, its child profiles and all progress permanently. Both are yours to choose, and both are set out on Closing or erasing your account.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for young children and is used with a parent or guardian's involvement. Because that raises specific obligations, it has its own page: Children's Privacy. Please read it alongside this one.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what is held about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deleting is available directly — see Deleting your account. For anything else, write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant — particularly anything that would mean collecting more than we do today — we will say so clearly in the app before it takes effect.
