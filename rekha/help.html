Help & troubleshooting

Rekha Learning · general questions are in the FAQ

I did not get the confirmation code

The code is six digits and arrives by email, usually within a minute.

  • Check the spam or promotions folder. This is by far the most common cause.
  • Check the address you typed for a small mistake.
  • Wait a minute before asking for another — requesting several in quick succession can trip a rate limit and delay them all. If that happens, wait an hour and try once.
  • Use the newest code. An older one stops working once a new one is sent.

Still nothing? Write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com and we will confirm the account manually.

“Something went wrong. Check your connection and try again.”

The app could not reach our servers. Check the device is online, then try again. If other apps work and this one does not, it is more likely to be us than you — wait a few minutes and retry. In the meantime you can keep using the app: tap Play as guest and everything works offline.

My child's progress did not save

  • Progress is only saved to an account when signed in. Guest progress stays on that device and does not transfer.
  • Make sure the right child profile is selected — each child's progress is kept separately, so it can look missing if another profile is active.
  • If the device was offline, progress is on the device and syncs when a connection returns. Open the app once with internet.

There is no sound

  • Check the device is not on silent and turn the media volume up — the volume buttons change different things depending on what is on screen, so change it while a lesson is playing.
  • Check nothing else is holding the audio, such as a paused video or a connected Bluetooth speaker that is out of range.
  • Background music can be turned off in the grown-up settings; spoken instructions are separate and always play.

The microphone is not hearing my child

Nothing is lost if it does not — the activity always finishes and always earns a star, and it can be skipped without penalty. If you would like it to work: allow the microphone permission when asked, try a quieter room, and note that speech recognition is genuinely unreliable for small voices. The app is deliberately built so that this never counts against your child.

Reminders are not arriving

  • Check the reminder is switched on in the grown-up settings and that the time is right.
  • Allow notifications for the app in your device's settings.
  • Remember that reminders are skipped on days your child has already practised — that is intended.
  • Battery-saver and “deep sleep” features on some Android phones delay scheduled notifications. Excluding the app from battery optimisation usually fixes it.

The app closed unexpectedly

Reopen it — finished activities are saved as they complete, so at most the current activity is lost. If it keeps happening, tell us at alpha.silent.official@gmail.com what your device is and what was on screen, and we will look into it.

The screen-time limit is not counting as I expect

It counts only time the app is actually open and in the foreground. Time with the app in the background, the screen off, or the tablet in a bag does not count. A lesson already started always finishes, so a little may be used past the limit — that is deliberate.

My child was placed at the wrong level

Change it in the grown-up settings under Starting class. Nothing is locked away either way: every earlier chapter stays on the map, and moving the start does not erase anything already done.

I forgot my password

Use the password reset on the sign-in screen, which sends a link to the account's email address. If you no longer have access to that address, write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com.

Still stuck

Write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com. Telling us the device, the Android version, and what was on screen when it went wrong makes it far quicker to fix.
