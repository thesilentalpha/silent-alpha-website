Data safety summary

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Rekha Learning · The detail is in the Privacy Policy

At a glance

  • Is any data collected? Only if you create an account. Guest play sends nothing.
  • Is data shared with third parties? No.
  • Is data sold? No.
  • Advertising or tracking? None in the app.
  • Encrypted in transit? Yes, over HTTPS.
  • Can you request deletion? Yes — see Deleting your account.

Collected with an account

  • Name and email address (grown-up) — to create and recover the account. Required.
  • Phone number (grown-up) — optional; used only as an alternative sign-in.
  • Username and password — to sign in.
  • Child's name and age — to greet them, tell profiles apart, and suggest a starting chapter. A nickname is enough.
  • App activity — lessons finished, stars, skill estimates, and per-activity records including tracing measurements, used to adapt difficulty and build the parent's report.
  • Avatar and handedness — preferences that shape the screen.

Not collected

  • Location of any kind — the app has no location permission.
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, or SMS.
  • Audio recordings. The microphone is used only inside a speaking activity, and nothing is stored or transmitted.
  • Advertising identifiers, or any identifier used to track a user across other apps and websites.
  • Payment information — there are no purchases in the app.

Device permissions and why

  • Internet — to sync progress when signed in. The app works without it.
  • Microphone — requested only the first time a child taps to speak in a speaking activity, never at launch. Refusing it skips that one activity type and nothing else.
  • Notifications — only if practice reminders are switched on by a grown-up. Reminders are scheduled on the device; none pass through a server.

Kept on the device only

Language, daily goal, screen-time limit and today's usage, and the reminder time. These never reach our servers.
