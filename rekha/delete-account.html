Closing or erasing your account

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Rekha Learning

Two different things, and the difference matters

You can close your account yourself, in the app, straight away. You can also ask us to erase it completely. Closing is instant, and reversible for thirty days — after which the account is erased automatically. Erasing on request is immediate to ask for, permanent, and goes through us. Both are described below, and you are entitled to either.

If you never made an account

Playing as a guest stores everything on the device only. Uninstalling the app removes it. There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing was ever sent.

Removing one child, keeping the account

Open the app, go to Grown-ups behind the parent gate, and remove that child's profile. Their profile and progress go; the account and any other children stay.

Closing your account, in the app

In the app: Grown-ups → Close account. You will be asked to type CLOSE to confirm, so that it cannot happen with a stray tap.

From that moment:

  • Signing in stops working.
  • Nothing further syncs, and none of your children's data can be read or written by the app any more. This is enforced by the database, not merely respected by the app.
  • Any leaderboard entry stops appearing.

For thirty days we keep the account, deactivated. We do this deliberately: closing an account is often done in a hurry, or by mistake, and a child's entire history should not be destroyed by one mis-tap. During those thirty days, write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com and we can reopen it.

After thirty days it is erased automatically, whether or not anyone asks — everything listed under “What erasing removes” below. So closing does get you to erasure; it just takes a month, and gives you that month to change your mind.

Two things follow from that, and both matter. Thirty days is a deadline on reopening — after it, there is nothing left to reopen. And for those thirty days closing is not yet erasing: the data still exists, unreachable but present. If you would rather not wait, ask us and we will erase it now.

Erasing your account completely

Write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com from the email address the account was created with, with the subject “Erase my account”. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need to have closed it first.

We will confirm we have received it, erase the account, and confirm again when it is done — within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We ask you to write from the account's own address because it is the simplest way to be sure the request is genuinely from the account holder. If you no longer have access to it, say so and we will find another way to verify.

What erasing removes

  • The account itself and its sign-in credentials.
  • The grown-up's name, email address, username, and phone number if one was given.
  • Every child profile on the account — names, ages, avatars and preferences.
  • All learning progress: lesson stars, skill estimates and the record of finished activities.
  • Any leaderboard handle and score rows.

This is permanent and cannot be undone. It does not touch anything stored on your own device — uninstall the app for that.

Side by side

  • Who does it — closing: you, in the app. Erasing: us, on your written request.
  • How long — closing: immediate. Erasing: within 30 days.
  • Can it be undone — closing: yes, for thirty days. Erasing: no.
  • Is the data still held — closing: for thirty days, deactivated and unreachable, then erased automatically. Erasing: no.

What may briefly remain after erasing

Encrypted backups of our database are kept for a short period for disaster recovery and are overwritten on a rolling basis, so erased records may persist there until those backups age out. They are not accessible for ordinary use and are never used to restore an individual erased account. We also keep a minimal record that a request was made and completed, because being able to show that we honoured it is the point of honouring it.

Just want a copy first?

Ask at the same address and we will send you what is held about your account before anything is removed.
