Deleting your account and data

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Rekha Learning

If you never made an account

Playing as a guest stores everything on the device only. Uninstalling the app removes it. There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing was ever sent.

Deleting one child, keeping the account

Open the app, go to Grown-ups behind the parent gate, and remove the child's profile. Their profile and progress are deleted; the account and any other children remain.

Deleting the whole account

Write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com from the email address the account was created with, with the subject “Delete my account”. You do not need to give a reason.

We will confirm we have received it, delete the account, and confirm again when it is done — within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We ask you to write from the account's own email address because it is the simplest way to be sure the request is really from the account holder; if you no longer have access to that address, say so and we will find another way to verify it.

What gets deleted

  • The account itself and its sign-in credentials.
  • The grown-up's name, email address, username and phone number if one was given.
  • Every child profile on the account — names, ages, avatars and preferences.
  • All learning progress: lesson stars, skill estimates and the record of finished activities.
  • Any leaderboard handle and score rows.

This is permanent and cannot be undone. It does not delete anything already stored on your own device — uninstall the app for that.

What may briefly remain

Encrypted backups of our database are kept for a short period for disaster recovery and are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted records may persist there until those backups age out. They are not accessible for ordinary use and are never used to restore an individual deleted account. We may also keep a minimal record that a deletion request was made and completed, because being able to show that we honoured it is the point of honouring it.

Just want a copy first?

Ask at the same address and we will send you what is held about your account before anything is removed.
