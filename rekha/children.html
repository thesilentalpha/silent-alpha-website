Children's Privacy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Rekha Learning · Read with the Privacy Policy

Why this page exists

Rekha Learning is made for children who are typically between three and ten years old. Laws in several countries set specific rules for services aimed at children — the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in India among them. This page sets out how we meet them and, more usefully, what we actually do.

The design decision underneath all of it

The app is built so that a child can use it fully without an account and without any data leaving the device. An account exists only to carry progress between devices and to let a parent see a report. If you would rather we held nothing at all about your child, use the app as a guest — nothing is withheld from you for doing so.

A parent creates the account, not the child

  • An account is created by an adult with their own email address, which must be confirmed with a code before the account works.
  • Child profiles can only be added from inside that adult's account.
  • Settings that matter — screen time, the leaderboard, the language, deleting data — sit behind a parent gate that asks a multiplication question a young child cannot answer.
  • A child is never asked for an email address, a phone number, or any way of contacting them.

What is held about a child

A name, an age, an avatar, a handedness preference, and their learning progress. That is all. A nickname is sufficient for the name and we would encourage one — the app only ever uses it to greet them and to tell two profiles apart.

We do not collect a child's contact details, photographs, location, contacts, device identifiers for advertising, or any persistent identifier used to track them across other apps or websites.

Things this app does not do

  • No advertising of any kind, and therefore no behavioural profiling or ad targeting.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking code.
  • No social features between children. There is no chat, no messaging, no friend list, no user-generated content and no way for one user to contact another.
  • No purchases inside the app, and so no way for a child to spend money.
  • No location. The app holds no location permission.
  • No selling or sharing of a child's data with anyone for their own purposes.

The leaderboard

The one feature that shows a child alongside others is off unless a grown-up turns it on. Even then, a child appears under a generated handle and never a real name, is grouped only with enough others that nobody can be identified, and is never shown last. The city or country used for grouping is typed in by the parent; nothing is detected.

The microphone

Where an activity asks a child to speak, recognition is requested on the device, the resulting text is compared in memory and immediately discarded, and no audio or transcript is stored or transmitted. The activity can always be skipped. The Privacy Policy describes the one platform limitation honestly.

Parental rights

As the parent or guardian of a child using this app, you may:

  • See everything held about your child — the in-app report shows their activity, and you can ask us for a full copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong, from within the app or by asking us.
  • Delete a child's profile and everything attached to it, or the whole account. See Deleting your account.
  • Refuse further collection — delete the account and keep using the app as a guest, which sends nothing anywhere.

To exercise any of these, write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com from the email address on the account. We will respond within 30 days, and we will not ask a child to do any of it.

If you believe a child's data was collected in error

Write to alpha.silent.official@gmail.com and tell us what happened. If we find that we hold data about a child that we should not, we will delete it and confirm to you that we have.
