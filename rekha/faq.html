Getting started

What age is it for?
Roughly three to ten. The early chapters are single strokes and shapes for a child who has never held a pencil; the later ones reach primary-school reading, writing and arithmetic. A child does not need to be able to read to start — every instruction is spoken.

Do I need an account?
No. You can tap “Play as guest” and use everything. The difference is that guest progress stays on that device and is not saved anywhere else — so it is lost if the app is uninstalled, and it does not follow your child to another tablet.

Why does it ask for my email?
To confirm the account is yours and to let you get back in if you forget the password. We send a six-digit code to that address; type it into the app to finish signing up. A phone number is optional.

Can I add more than one child?
Yes, up to ten on one account. Each has their own progress, stars, awards and starting point, and switching between them does not mix anything up.

What is the starting-point check?
When you add a child, the app suggests a chapter based on their age, then offers a short game-like check of a few questions to place them better. It only ever moves a child up to a level they have actually demonstrated, never down, and you can skip it and keep the age-based guess. Earlier chapters always stay on the map whatever it decides.

Using the app day to day

How do I get to the grown-up settings?
Tap the padlock in the corner and answer the multiplication question. It is deliberately something a young child cannot do, not a real password.

What is the ring at the top of the map?
Today's goal. It fills as your child finishes activities and turns into a tick when they reach it. Beside it, a flame shows their run of consecutive days. Missing a day costs nothing — the run simply starts again, and the app never says anything about a day that was missed.

Can I change or turn off the daily goal?
Yes, in the grown-up settings: 3, 5, 10, 15 activities, or off. With it off, the streak still runs.

What is the trophy?
The awards cabinet. It appears once your child has earned their first award and shows a dot when something new is inside. Awards are earned for things already done and can never be taken away again.

How do screen-time limits work?
Set a daily limit in the grown-up settings (15 to 60 minutes). The app counts only the time it is actually open and in front of your child — not time spent in a bag or in the background. When the time is up your child sees a gentle “all done for today” rather than being locked out mid-lesson: a lesson already started always finishes. You can grant ten more minutes, or reset today's count, from the same screen.

Are reminders sent to my child?
No. The practice reminder is a note to you, at a time you choose, and only on days your child has not already practised. If they have played that day, it is not sent.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. The whole curriculum and every spoken line are inside the app. Signed-in progress syncs when a connection is next available; nothing is lost while offline.

Can I change the language?
The grown-up screens are available in English and Hindi. Your child's lessons are spoken in English for now.

Progress and reports

Where do I see how my child is doing?
In the grown-up settings: lessons finished, stars, activities, days practised, time spent, and a chapter-by-chapter view of where they are.

My child got a low score. Should I worry?
Probably not. Tracing scores reflect fine motor control, which is exactly what a young child is still developing, and the app is designed so that finishing always earns something. It is a practice aid, not an assessment — see the note in our Terms.

Can my child fail or get stuck?
No. There is no failure state anywhere. Every completed activity earns at least one star, nothing shows a cross, and no chapter already reached is ever taken away.

What is the leaderboard, and is it safe?
It is off unless you turn it on. If you do, your child appears under a made-up handle such as “SwiftMangoTiger”, never their real name, and only within a group large enough that nobody can be identified. Nobody is ever shown last. It uses a city or country you type in yourself — the app has no location permission.

Privacy and safety

Are there ads?
No. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing to buy inside the app.

Can my child talk to anyone in the app?
No. There is no chat, no messaging and no way for users to contact each other.

Does it record my child's voice?
Only inside an activity that asks them to say a word, and only after they tap to start. The word is checked on the device and immediately discarded — no audio is saved and nothing is sent to us. It can always be skipped. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do I delete everything?
See Deleting your account.

Cost

Is it free?
Yes, and there are no purchases inside it. If that ever changes, we will say so clearly before it does.
