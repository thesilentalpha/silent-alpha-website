Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to thesilentalpha.com only

The short version

This website is a placeholder while we finish building two apps. It doesn't have accounts, forms, cookies, or analytics — there is nothing on this site for you to sign up for or hand data to. The one interactive element is a "Contact us" email link, which simply opens your own email app; nothing is collected or stored by this website itself.

What this site does and doesn't do

We don't run analytics or advertising scripts on this site, and we don't set cookies. Like effectively any website, our hosting provider's servers keep standard technical logs of requests (e.g. IP address, browser type, timestamp) purely for security and reliability — this is infrastructure-level logging we don't access or use for anything, not data we collect ourselves.

The apps themselves

Rekha Learning and SilentAlpha are separate products, each with their own data practices, and each will publish its own detailed privacy policy — covering exactly what it collects, why, and how it's protected — before or at the point it becomes available to the public. Nothing on this teaser page should be read as describing either app's actual privacy practices once it launches.

Since Rekha Learning is aimed at children, its own privacy policy will be written with COPPA and similar children's-privacy requirements specifically in mind, including how much (or little) is ever collected about a child versus a parent.

Children's privacy (this site)

This website itself is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, child or adult.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as the site or the apps evolve. Since this is currently just a coming-soon page, expect this policy to be replaced with app-specific ones as each product launches.

Contact

Questions about this policy — reach us at alpha.silent.official@gmail.com, or see the Contact page.
